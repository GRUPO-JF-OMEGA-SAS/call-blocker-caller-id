FAQ
Is Call Blocker free?

Yes, completely free with all features unlocked. The app is supported by ads.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app works immediately — no login, no registration required.

What permissions does the app need?

As a default phone and SMS app, it needs access to calls, messages, and contacts to function properly. All data is processed on-device.

Can I switch back to my old phone app?

Yes, anytime. Go to Android Settings → Default apps → Phone app and select your preferred dialer.
