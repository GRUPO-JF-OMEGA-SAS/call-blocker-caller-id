Full Description (2937/4000)

Call Blocker: Caller ID is a phone and messaging app that helps you take control of unwanted calls. Block spam callers, see who is calling, and manage your calls and text messages in one place. IMPORTANT: this app works as your default phone app and your default SMS app. To block incoming calls, show caller ID and handle messages, Android asks you to set Call Blocker: Caller ID as the default Phone app and default SMS app. You choose this on a clear set-default screen after the short introduction, and you can switch back to another app in Android settings at any time. CALL BLOCKER - Block any number straight from your recent calls or from a call's detail screen - Calls from blocked numbers are screened and stopped before they ring - Review and manage your blocked numbers list, and unblock a number whenever you like - A simple way to deal with spam callers, scam calls and nuisance numbers you do not want to hear from CALLER ID - See the caller's name from your own contacts the moment a call comes in - Clear incoming call screen with answer and decline buttons - Know who is calling before you pick up DIALER AND RECENTS - Easy dialpad for placing calls - Recents list of your call history, so you can call back, check details or block a number - Missed call notifications MESSAGES (DEFAULT SMS APP) - Read your SMS and MMS conversations in clean message threads - Compose and send new text messages - Receive new messages and get notified when they arrive SETTINGS AND LANGUAGES - Choose your app language - Manage blocked numbers - Rate, share and check for updates - Privacy Policy link inside the app WHY THE APP ASKS FOR PERMISSIONS Each permission is used only for the feature described. Your call log, contacts and messages stay on your device and are used to run the app's features. - Phone, call log (READ_CALL_LOG, READ_PHONE_STATE, READ_PHONE_NUMBERS): show your recent calls, detect incoming calls and let you block numbers from your history. - Make and answer calls (CALL_PHONE, ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS): place calls from the dialpad and answer calls from the incoming call screen. - Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): match incoming numbers and message senders to contact names for caller ID. - SMS and MMS (READ_SMS, SEND_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS, RECEIVE_MMS): receive, show and send your text messages as the default SMS app. - Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): alert you to missed calls and new messages. PRIVACY No account, sign-up or login is needed, and all features are available right away. The app is free and shows ads, supported by Google AdMob and ad mediation partners; the ads use an advertising ID, and you can review the consent choices shown in the app. Please read the Privacy Policy before use: https://grupo-jf-omega-sas.github.io/call-blocker-caller-id/privacy/ Download Call Blocker: Caller ID to block unwanted calls, see who is calling and keep your calls and messages together in one phone app.
