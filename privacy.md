This Privacy Policy explains what information Call Blocker Caller ID (the "App") accesses, collects, uses and shares, and the choices you have. The App is a caller ID, call blocking, phone dialer and SMS messaging app for Android. By using the App you acknowledge the practices described here.

## 1. Summary

- **Your call log, contacts and text messages stay on your device.** The App reads them locally to identify callers, block calls and show your conversations. We do not upload them to our servers, and we do not operate a server that receives them.
- **The App has no account or sign-in.** You do not create a profile, and you do not upload user-generated content.
- **The App shows ads and uses analytics and crash reporting.** To do this, third-party SDKs (Google AdMob, Firebase, AppLovin, Meta, Adjust and TikTok) collect device-level and usage data such as your advertising ID, approximate region, device model and app interactions. This is described in Sections 4 to 6.
- **You control consent and advertising settings.** Where required by law, we ask for your consent through a consent message, and you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

## 2. Information the App accesses on your device

The App is designed to work as your default phone app and default SMS app. Android only grants the permissions below to the extent you allow them. The information is used to provide the features described and is not transmitted to us.

| Information / permission | Why the App uses it |
|---|---|
| **Contacts** (`READ_CONTACTS`) | To match an incoming or outgoing number against your address book in real time, so the App can show the caller's name and show contact names in your call history and messages. |
| **Call log** (`READ_CALL_LOG`) | To display your recent calls, missed calls and blocked calls, and to let you call back or block a number. Used only when the App is set as your default phone app. |
| **Phone state and phone numbers** (`READ_PHONE_STATE`, `READ_PHONE_NUMBERS`) | To detect when a call is ringing, active or ended, and to show the phone number of your SIM. |
| **Making and answering calls** (`CALL_PHONE`, `ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS`) | To place a call when you tap Call, and to answer or end a call from the in-call screen. |
| **SMS and MMS** (`READ_SMS`, `SEND_SMS`, `RECEIVE_SMS`, `RECEIVE_MMS`) | To receive, display and send your text and picture messages. Used only when the App is set as your default SMS app. |
| **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) | To notify you of missed calls and new messages. |
| **Network state** (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`) | To load ads, send analytics and crash reports, and fetch app configuration. |
| **Advertising ID** (`AD_ID`, `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID`, `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION`) | To serve and measure ads and to measure app installs. See Sections 4 to 6. |

**Call blocking and screening.** When a call comes in, the App checks the number against your contacts and your own block list on the device and decides whether to ring, silence or reject the call. This decision is made locally.

**We do not access** your precise or approximate location, photos, files, microphone recordings or other apps installed on your device.

## 3. Information we do not collect

- We do not require or collect your name, email address or any account details.
- We do not collect the content of your calls, contacts or messages on our servers.
- We do not sell your call log, contacts or messages.

## 5. Advertising

The App is supported by advertising. Ads are served by Google AdMob and may be fulfilled by partner ad networks through mediation. These companies may use your advertising ID, IP address and information about your interactions with ads to select and show ads, limit how often you see an ad, measure performance and prevent fraud. Depending on your settings and consent, ads may be personalized.

Ads may be displayed on the App's screens, including screens outside of call or message content. We do not place ads based on the content of your calls, contacts or messages.

**Your choices:**

- On Android, open **Settings > Google > Ads** (the path may vary by device) to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
- Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions), the App shows a consent message when you first use it. You can change your choice later through the privacy options in the App, if available, or by clearing the App's data to see the message again.
- You can also manage ad preferences at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).

## 8. Data storage, retention and security

- **On your device:** Call history, block lists, preferences and message data used by the App are stored locally on your device. You can remove them by clearing the App's data, uninstalling the App, or deleting individual items in the App. Messages and call history in Android's system databases remain under your device's control.
- **Third parties:** Data collected by Firebase, AdMob, mediation partners, Adjust, Meta and TikTok is retained under their own retention policies. Analytics and crash data are generally retained for a limited period set by the provider.
- **Security:** We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for network communication by the App. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Because the App has no account and we do not hold your call, contact or message data, there is no account or personal profile for us to delete. To limit data held by third-party SDKs, you can reset your advertising ID, opt out of personalized ads and uninstall the App. You can also contact us (Section 12) and we will help direct your request to the relevant provider.

## 9. Default phone and SMS app

To work as a complete dialer and messaging app, the App asks you to set it as your default phone app and default SMS app. Android requires this before an app can access the call log and send and receive SMS. You can change your default apps at any time in Android **Settings > Apps > Default apps**. If you remove the App as the default, it will no longer receive calls or messages as the default handler, and some features will stop working.
